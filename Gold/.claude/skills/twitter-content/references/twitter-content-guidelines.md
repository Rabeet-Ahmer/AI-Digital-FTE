# Twitter/X Content Guidelines

## Brand Voice on Twitter

### Tone
- **Concise and punchy** — every word earns its place
- **Conversational** — more casual than LinkedIn, less casual than personal texting
- **Opinionated** — Twitter rewards strong perspectives (not controversial, just clear)
- **Real-time** — tie content to current events, trends, conversations

### Language
- Short sentences and fragments are fine
- Active voice mandatory
- No corporate jargon — "We shipped it" not "We facilitated the deployment"
- Numbers > adjectives: "40% faster" not "much faster"
- Abbreviations OK where natural: "info", "dev", "AI"

## Tweet Types

### High-Performing Formats

| Type | When | Example |
|------|------|---------|
| Hot take | Share a strong opinion | "The best code is the code you delete." |
| Metric share | Celebrate wins with data | "Just crossed 10K users. Here's the one thing that changed everything:" |
| Question | Drive engagement | "What's the tool you can't live without?" |
| Tip | Share practical value | "Tip: Always test your API with the worst-case payload first." |
| Thread | Deep dive on a topic | "5 lessons from scaling to $1M ARR (thread):" |

### Avoid
- Vague motivation: "Hustle harder!" — empty calories
- Self-promotion without value: "Check out our product!" — always lead with value
- Thread-baiting: "This thread will change your life" — don't overpromise
- Hashtag spam: Max 2-3 per tweet

## Hashtag Strategy

### Per Tweet
- **Single tweet:** 0-2 hashtags
- **Thread last tweet:** 2-3 hashtags
- Place at end of tweet

### Recommended Hashtags
- Industry: `#AI`, `#Tech`, `#SaaS`, `#Startup`
- Content: `#BuildInPublic`, `#FounderLife`, `#ProductDev`
- Growth: `#GrowthHacking`, `#BusinessTips`, `#Leadership`

## Thread Best Practices

1. **Hook tweet** must work standalone — it appears in the timeline without context
2. **Number your tweets** (1/, 2/, etc.) — helps readers track position
3. **End with a CTA** — "Follow for more", "Bookmark this thread", "What would you add?"
4. **Keep to 3-7 tweets** — sweet spot for engagement
5. **Each tweet adds new value** — no filler or transitions just to pad the thread

## Engagement Tactics

- Reply to comments within 1 hour for algorithm boost
- Quote tweet others' content with your take (more reach than RT)
- Pin your best-performing tweet for profile visitors
- Post consistently: 1-3 tweets per day, 2-3 threads per week

## Rate Limits (Free Tier)

- **1,500 tweets per month** (posting)
- **Reads:** Limited on free tier
- Track usage to avoid hitting limits mid-month
- Budget: ~50 tweets/day max, aim for 3-5 quality tweets/day

## Posting Schedule

| Time Slot | Best For | Day |
|-----------|----------|-----|
| 9:00 AM | Morning insight / news reaction | Mon-Fri |
| 12:00 PM | Midday hot take / question | Tue-Thu |
| 5:00 PM | End-of-day thread / recap | Wed-Fri |

- Avoid weekends unless reacting to breaking news
- Avoid holidays
- Space tweets at least 2 hours apart

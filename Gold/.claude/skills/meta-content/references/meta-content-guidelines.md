# Meta Content Guidelines (Facebook & Instagram)

## Brand Voice

### Tone Across Platforms
- **Facebook:** Conversational, storytelling-friendly, community-focused
- **Instagram:** Visual-first, punchy, hashtag-rich, aspirational but authentic
- Both: Professional but approachable, specific over vague, first-person perspective

### Language Rules
- Active voice always
- Use numbers and specifics: "grew revenue 40% in Q1" not "had a great quarter"
- No buzzwords: "synergy", "leverage", "ecosystem", "paradigm shift"
- Emojis: sparingly on Facebook (0-3), moderately on Instagram (3-5)

## Facebook Guidelines

### Post Types & Structure

| Type | Structure | Ideal Length |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Business Update | Result → Journey → What's Next | 200-400 words |
| Thought Leadership | Observation → Analysis → Takeaway | 150-300 words |
| Behind the Scenes | Setup → Story → Reflection | 200-500 words |
| Event/Announcement | What → When → Why It Matters → CTA | 100-200 words |
| Product Update | Problem → Solution → How to Get It | 150-300 words |

### Facebook Best Practices
- Posts with images get 2.3x more engagement
- Questions drive comments
- Native video outperforms links to YouTube
- Posting frequency: 3-5x per week
- Best times: Wednesday-Friday, 1-4 PM

### Facebook Hashtag Strategy
- Use 1-3 hashtags maximum
- Place at end of post
- Focus on branded hashtags and broad categories
- Examples: `#CompanyName`, `#Business`, `#Innovation`

## Instagram Guidelines

### Caption Structure
1. **Hook** (first ~125 chars visible): Question, bold statement, or story opener
2. **Body** (main content): 3-5 short paragraphs with line breaks
3. **CTA**: One clear ask (comment, save, share, link in bio)
4. **Separator**: Use `.` on separate lines to push hashtags down
5. **Hashtags**: 15-25 relevant hashtags

### Instagram Hashtag Strategy

#### Size Mix (for each post)
- 5-7 **niche** hashtags (10K-100K posts): `#StartupFounderLife`, `#AIBusiness`
- 5-7 **medium** hashtags (100K-500K posts): `#BusinessGrowth`, `#TechStartup`
- 3-5 **broad** hashtags (500K+ posts): `#Entrepreneur`, `#Business`, `#AI`

#### Categories to Include
- Industry: `#TechIndustry`, `#AIinBusiness`, `#SaaS`
- Role: `#CEO`, `#Founder`, `#Entrepreneur`
- Content type: `#BusinessTips`, `#LessonsLearned`, `#BehindTheScenes`
- Branded: `#CompanyName`, `#CompanyNameTeam`

### Instagram Image Requirements
- Must be a **publicly accessible URL** (Instagram API fetches the image)
- Recommended: 1080x1080px (square), 1080x1350px (portrait), 1080x566px (landscape)
- Supported formats: JPEG, PNG
- No text-heavy images (Instagram deprioritizes them)

## Content Calendar Suggestions

| Day | Facebook | Instagram |
|-----|----------|-----------|
| Monday | Industry insight / Week ahead | Motivational quote + team photo |
| Tuesday | Thought leadership article | Behind-the-scenes / process |
| Wednesday | Business update / metrics | Product feature highlight |
| Thursday | Team spotlight / culture | Customer story / testimonial |
| Friday | Weekend reading / recap | Milestone / celebration |

## Cross-Platform Rules

1. **Don't duplicate verbatim.** Same topic, different treatment per platform.
2. **Facebook favors depth.** Longer stories, more context, links to articles.
3. **Instagram favors visually paired content.** Every post needs an image.
4. **Hashtag discipline.** 1-3 on Facebook, 15-25 on Instagram.
5. **CTA differs.** Facebook: "Comment below" / "Share". Instagram: "Save this" / "Link in bio" / "Tag someone".

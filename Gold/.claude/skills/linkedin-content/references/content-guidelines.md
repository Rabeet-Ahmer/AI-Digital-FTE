# LinkedIn Content Guidelines

## Brand Voice

### Tone
- **Professional but approachable** — not stiff corporate-speak, not overly casual
- **Confident but humble** — share wins without bragging, share learnings from failures
- **Specific and actionable** — every post should teach something or spark discussion
- **First-person perspective** — "I", "we", "our team" — never third person

### Language
- Active voice always
- Short sentences preferred
- No buzzwords: "synergy", "leverage", "ecosystem", "paradigm shift", "disrupt"
- Replace jargon with plain language unless the audience shares the jargon
- Use numbers and specifics: "grew 40%" not "grew significantly"

### Personality Traits to Convey
- Builder / operator mindset
- Curious and always learning
- Values transparency and authenticity
- Cares about the team and people behind the work
- Thinks in systems and first principles

## Topic Guidelines

### Strong Topics (Post About These)
| Category | Examples |
|----------|---------|
| Business updates | Milestones, launches, partnerships, growth metrics |
| Lessons learned | Failures, pivots, surprises, behind-the-scenes |
| Industry insights | Trends, analysis, predictions with reasoning |
| Team & culture | Hiring, team wins, values in action, remote work |
| Professional growth | Skills learned, books, frameworks, mental models |
| AI & technology | Practical applications, tools, workflow improvements |

### Avoid These Topics
- **Politics or religion** — unless directly relevant to business (e.g., regulation)
- **Competitor bashing** — acknowledge competitors respectfully or not at all
- **Vague motivation** — "Hustle harder", "Dream big" — empty inspiration with no substance
- **Humble-brags** — "I'm so blessed that [flex]" — just share the achievement directly
- **Personal drama** — keep personal content professional

## Post Type Templates

### Business Update
- **Structure:** Result → how we got there → what's next
- **Example hook:** "We just hit [milestone]. Here's the 3 things that got us here."

### Thought Leadership
- **Structure:** Observation → analysis → actionable takeaway
- **Example hook:** "Everyone's talking about [trend]. Here's what they're missing."

### Industry Insight
- **Structure:** Data point or trend → what it means → what to do about it
- **Example hook:** "[Stat] of [audience] are doing [thing]. Is yours?"

### Milestone
- **Structure:** Achievement → team recognition → what's next
- **Example hook:** "[Number]. That's how many [things] we've [done] this [period]."

### Hiring
- **Structure:** Why we're hiring → what the role is → why it matters → how to apply
- **Example hook:** "We're looking for someone who [specific trait]. Here's why."

## Hashtag Strategy

### Always Include (Pick 1-2)
- `#Business` or `#Entrepreneurship`
- `#Leadership` or `#Management`
- `#Technology` or `#AI`

### Rotate Based on Topic (Pick 2-3)
- Business: `#StartupLife`, `#GrowthMindset`, `#BusinessStrategy`
- Tech: `#AIinBusiness`, `#Automation`, `#FutureOfWork`
- Team: `#Hiring`, `#TeamCulture`, `#RemoteWork`
- Learning: `#ProfessionalDevelopment`, `#LifelongLearning`, `#BookRecommendation`

### Rules
- 3-5 hashtags per post (not more)
- Place at the end, after the CTA
- No hashtag walls (#this #is #annoying #and #spammy)

## Formatting Best Practices

- **Line breaks matter** — LinkedIn collapses text. Use blank lines between paragraphs.
- **First 2 lines are critical** — they show before "see more". Hook must be here.
- **Emojis sparingly** — 0-2 per post. Use as bullet points if at all, not for decoration.
- **Lists and bullets** work well for readability
- **Tag people/companies** mentioned in the post for reach

## Posting Cadence

- **Target:** 2-3 posts per week
- **Best times:** Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM or 12-1 PM local time
- **Avoid:** Weekends, late evenings, holidays
- **Never post more than once per day** — it dilutes engagement
